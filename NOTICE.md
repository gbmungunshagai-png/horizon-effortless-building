This mod is a port of **Effortless Building** by Requios
(https://github.com/Requios/effortless-building, branch `1.21`) to Horizon.

- The build mode, modifier, radial menu and modifier screen logic in `src/` is translated from the
  original Java source, which its `neoforge.mods.toml` declares under the GNU Lesser General Public
  License. The licence text shipped with that repository is in `LICENSE-EffortlessBuilding.txt`.
- `assets/effortless/textures/gui/icons.png`, `widgets.png` and `modifiers.png` are the original
  mod's textures; `create_icons.png` is Create's icon sheet as bundled by Effortless Building.
  `assets/effortless/textures/gui/license.txt` is the original's note on their licence (MIT for
  `icons.png` and `widgets.png`).
